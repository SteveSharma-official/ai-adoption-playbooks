# Writing Standard (all books and labs)

- Australian English.
- No em dashes.
- Every chapter opens with a real enterprise problem, surfaces the pain, moves to practical implementation and ends with a decision.
- Scenarios are fictional and labelled as illustrative. No invented case studies, testimonials or measured outcomes.
- Quantitative claims carry source, organisation, date, context and how the figure was measured.
- Durable method lives in the book; volatile platform detail lives in the lab repository and the platform change log.
- Labs use synthetic data only and never report results that were not executed.
